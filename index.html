Species guide 01

October Walleye Strategy

At Chatfield, test accessible structure and depth changes; at ponds, observe bank access and signs of fish activity.

  • Where to start: At Chatfield: test points, weed edges, and depth transitions where accessible. At ponds: start along safe bank edges and visible structure.
  • When to try: Dawn, dusk, and other low-light periods can be useful starting points.
  • What to try: Jig-and-minnow presentations, legal live-bait rigs, or hard-plastic lures.
  • How to adjust: Test more than one depth and change one variable at a time.
Species guide 02

October Common Carp Strategy

At smaller ponds, look for feeding signs near accessible edges and keep your approach quiet; confirm carp are present before targeting them.

  • Where to look: At ponds: accessible shallows and soft-bottom areas where feeding activity is observed. At Chatfield, confirm carp presence and select a suitable legal area.
  • Bait: Consider familiar carp baits, including corn where lawful.
  • Presentation: Place bait accurately and avoid unnecessary disturbance.
  • Cooler water: Activity may change; observe patiently and adjust your plan.
